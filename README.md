# WorkLog

## Retired on 7 October 2026

This repository is archived and no longer under active development. Personal engineering writing now lives in [The Software Engineer’s Notebook](https://namestarlit.com/notebook/) on my portfolio.

WorkLog began as a Flask and MongoDB prototype for recording completed and planned work. I considered expanding it with project links, milestones, evidence attachments, and a NestJS backend, but that expansion was not implemented.

The need I chose to pursue was explaining engineering problems, decisions, and lessons. Extending the portfolio's existing Eleventy and Markdown workflow gave that writing a home alongside the projects it describes, without a separate application, account system, or database.

The notebook serves my personal writing. It does not replace WorkLog's task management features or migrate accounts and work records. The original source and history remain here for reference.

- [View the retired project case study](https://namestarlit.com/w/worklog/)

## Original project overview

nscworklog -> a software for logging work done in detail.
Do you remember the work you did yesterday? Probably yes. Do you remember what you worked on last week? How much do you remember in detail?
Do you remember what you worked on last year? What impact did it make? What was your contribution to that impact?

If you don't have clear answers to any of the questions above, that's what nscworklog is made for. You can log your work today for easy reference anytime.
Also, It's an easy way to take facts to put on your resume.
